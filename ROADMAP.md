# MathProject roadmap

Agreed with Abed on 2026-10-25. Goal: students enjoy coming back, not only
"solve and get bored". Order can change; items marked MUST are not optional.

## Now
1. **Math keypad** (live, on for everyone on phones; admin → Settings can switch it off). Our own keypad for answers on phones,
   instead of the iPhone/Android keyboard. Must not hide the question or
   squeeze the screen. Expert UI/UX work.

## Next: setup
2. **Finish all subjects / modules** (Sulaiman's content).
3. **Shared exercise engine** — steps, checking, scoring built once, so new
   topics are quick and every improvement reaches all topics.

## Then: student experience
4. **Student profile** — achievements, progress per topic, and a beautiful
   avatar. Students earn points and spend them on avatar items.
5. **Hints in levels** (later) — nudge → bigger clue → show the step, and
   spotting common mistakes ("you forgot to flip the sign").
6. **Touch the math** (careful) — drag a term across "=", balance scale,
   draggable graphs, rotating 3D solids. Only where it's easy on a phone:
   big touch targets, no tiny drags, always a tap alternative.

## MUST, after setup and all modules are done
7. **Challenges with friends and class.**
8. **Leaderboard** across all students, filtered by school and grade.

## Business
9. **Payments** — monthly subscription (admin "Sales" page is ready for it).

## Other ideas we liked (not scheduled)
- Learning map per grade with 1–3 stars per topic.
- Daily streak + daily 5-minute challenge.
- Test-prep mode (timed mix) and "review my mistakes".
- Parent weekly summary.
- "Add to home screen" prompt (icon is ready).
